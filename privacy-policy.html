International Data Transfers

Some third-party providers we use — such as Meta, Cloudflare, and Supabase — may process or store information on servers located outside India. These providers offer services globally and may transfer data to jurisdictions where their infrastructure is maintained.

Where such transfers occur, the processing of your information is governed by the respective provider's privacy policy and applicable laws. We rely on providers who commit to recognized data protection standards where available.

If you have questions about how a specific provider handles your data, we encourage you to review their privacy policy or contact us for more information.

06

WhatsApp Business Platform Usage

Makwall Products uses the Meta WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) to send automated messages on behalf of our clients. These messages are triggered by real business events — such as payment reminders, appointment confirmations, order updates, and renewal notifications.

Opt-in: Messages are only sent to end users who have explicitly opted in to receive communications from our clients. We do not send unsolicited messages.

Opt-out: Recipients may opt out of receiving WhatsApp messages using the opt-out mechanism provided by the respective business.

Data handling: WhatsApp numbers and message content are processed through Meta's infrastructure in accordance with Meta's privacy policy. We do not use WhatsApp data for purposes other than delivering the automation service our clients have requested.

07

Payment Information

All payment transactions are processed through Razorpay, a PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway. We do not collect, store, or process credit card numbers, debit card numbers, or bank account details on our servers.

Payment information submitted during transactions is handled directly by Razorpay under their privacy policy and security standards. We may retain basic billing information (invoice number, transaction ID, date, amount) for accounting and tax purposes as required by Indian law.

08

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These include:

  • Secure data storage via Supabase with row-level security and encrypted connections
  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmitted to and from our website
  • Cloudflare security features including DDoS protection and web application firewall
  • Restricted access to client data on a need-to-know basis

No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach, we will respond appropriately in accordance with applicable legal obligations.

09

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Client project data: Retained for the duration of the service agreement plus a reasonable period thereafter for backup and legal compliance purposes. Clients may request deletion of their data after project completion.

Enquiry and contact form data: Retained as long as reasonably needed to address the enquiry, unless a client relationship is established.

Financial and accounting records: May be retained for the period required under applicable Indian laws.
