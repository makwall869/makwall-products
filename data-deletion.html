How to Request Deletion Customer Data WhatsApp Assets Connected Accounts Logs & Backups Third-Party Providers Verification & Processing Time

This page explains how Clients and end users can request the deletion of their personal data from Makwall Products' systems. We respect your right to control your data and will process deletion requests in accordance with applicable laws.

We are an MSME registered software services provider (NIC 58201) based in Mangalagiri, Andhra Pradesh, India.

01

How to Request Deletion

To request deletion of your personal data, send an email to:

makwallproducts@gmail.com

Subject: Data Deletion Request

Please include the following in your request:

  • Your full name and business name
  • Registered email address or phone number used with our services
  • Specific details about the data you want deleted
  • Any relevant project or account identifiers
02

Customer Data

Upon receiving a valid deletion request, we will delete the following types of data stored in our systems:

CRM Records
Contacts
WhatsApp Connections
Projects
Lead Information
Contact Form Submissions
03

WhatsApp Assets

Makwall Products cannot delete Meta-owned assets — such as WhatsApp message templates, business account details, or phone number registrations — without the Client's authorization. These assets are owned and controlled by the Client's Meta Business Account.

To delete WhatsApp assets:

  • The Client must provide explicit authorization to Makwall to disconnect or delete WhatsApp assets
  • Alternatively, the Client can manage these assets directly through their Meta Business Account
  • Once authorized, we will remove WhatsApp configuration and stored data from our systems

Note: Deletion of assets on Meta's side is subject to Meta's own processes and timelines.

04

Connected Accounts

If a Client disconnects or requests disconnection, we will remove:

Access Tokens
Webhook Subscriptions
Stored IDs
Business IDs
Phone Number IDs
05

Logs & Backups

Some logs may be retained for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Security — logs related to authentication and access attempts
  • Fraud prevention — records of suspicious or anomalous activity
  • Legal compliance — records required to be retained under applicable law
  • Audit — logs of data access and system changes

Backups: Deletion from active systems does not immediately remove data from backup systems. Data in backups will be overwritten during the regular backup rotation cycle, which may take additional time.

06

Third-Party Providers

Deletion of data may also involve the following third-party providers used in our service delivery. Each provider has its own deletion processes and timelines:

Supabase Database & backend
Meta WhatsApp & messaging
Google Workspace, Sheets, Apps Script
Brevo Transactional email
Cloudflare CDN & security

Where data is stored with third-party providers, we will coordinate deletion with the provider where possible. Clients may also contact these providers directly for data deletion requests.

07

Verification & Processing Time

Identity Verification: Before processing any deletion request, we will verify the identity of the requestor to ensure the data is being deleted by the authorized person or business. We may request additional information to confirm identity.

Processing Time: Once identity is verified, we will process the deletion request within 5 to 10 business days. Complex requests involving multiple systems or third-party providers may take longer.

We will confirm once the deletion is complete. If we are unable to delete certain data due to legal or operational requirements, we will explain the reason in our response.
