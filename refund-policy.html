Refund & Cancellation Policy

How cancellations and refunds work for Makwall AI services.

Last updated: October 31, 2026

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to all payments made to Makwall AI, the trade name of Badukunda Mohan Kumar (Proprietor), GSTIN 37QBSPK4892K1ZB, for our services: AI agents, websites, Gym CRM, WhatsApp automation, email automation and custom software. All our services are digital.

2. Setup and project fees

  • If you cancel before we start work, you get a full refund of the setup or project fee.
  • Once work has started, setup and project fees are non-refundable, because custom work begins immediately.
  • If we are unable to deliver what was agreed in your quote, we refund the amount for the part not delivered.

3. Monthly and prepaid plans

  • You can cancel a monthly plan at any time by email or WhatsApp. The cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and you will not be charged again.
  • We do not refund the billing period that is already running.
  • For prepaid plans (6 months, 1 year or 2 years), you can stop renewal at any time. Refunds for unused time are not provided, except where required by law.

4. Third-party costs

Costs paid to other companies on your behalf, such as domain names, WhatsApp (Meta) messaging charges, SMS or email credits and app store fees, are not refundable once paid.

5. Duplicate or failed payments

If you were charged twice by mistake, or money was deducted for a failed payment, we refund the extra amount in full.

6. How to request a refund or cancel

Email mohan@makwall.com or message us on WhatsApp with your name, phone number and payment details. We reply within 2 working days.

7. Refund timeline

Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 working days to the original payment method (UPI, card, net banking or wallet). Your bank may take a few more days to show the amount.
