About Makwall Products

Software for the work that shouldn't need a human every time.

Makwall Products is a software company that helps businesses automate repetitive work, improve customer communication, and manage operations digitally. We specialize in WhatsApp automation, AI chatbots, custom CRM development, business websites, workflow automation, business dashboards, AI integrations, and internal management systems.

We work directly with the businesses we build for — gyms, clinics, tuition centers, repair shops, and retailers — which means every system we ship is shaped by an actual front desk, not a generic template. If a workflow doesn't hold up on a busy Monday morning, we don't ship it.
